What to review

  • Is the seven-screen first run the right length, or should instrument review move to Home?
  • Should pending instruments block totals, or keep importing and tag affected transactions?
  • Should unparsed SMS get a dedicated list, or is the count in Import enough?
  • Is the before/after category story clear: Home pre-correction, Insights post-correction?
  • Are SAR and EGP separate totals sufficient, with no conversion shown?
  • Should CSV export ever include the source SMS text, or only transactions and corrections?
